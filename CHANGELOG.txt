BLINDSPOT CHANGELOG
===================

2026.9.7 - 25 September 2026

Features implemented:
- On Windows, the playback device list can send BlindSpot's own player to a
  chosen sound card, or back to the Windows default.
- Recently Played includes songs and episodes played in BlindSpot for 20
  seconds, merged with Spotify's history newest first without duplicates.
  Spotify itself only lists songs that played to the end.

Bugs fixed:
- Returning to the transcript window with Alt+Tab puts the screen reader back
  in the transcript. The transcript now uses the same plain text box as the
  Lyrics window, whose focus screen readers follow reliably.
- Following playback and playing from a line in a transcript now land on the
  right line. Positions used to drift by one character per line, so in long
  transcripts they could fall on the line before.
- Refreshing Recently Played now moves to the newest play, instead of
  staying on the previous song and leaving new plays unseen above it.


2026.9.6 - 25 September 2026

Features implemented:
- Platform-independent OPML podcast subscription import and direct RSS audio
  streaming through BlindSpot's existing playback and seek controls.
- Optional podcast transcripts from publisher feeds, with an on-demand local
  Whisper fallback, synchronized playback navigation, accessible cancellation
  and portable caching.
- Local audio-file transcription from the File menu using the same accessible
  transcript reader, synchronized playback controls and portable Whisper tools.
- Optional DeepL translation for podcast and local-audio transcripts, with
  Original/Translation switching, preserved timed navigation and local caching.
- Rewrote the manual in plainer, friendlier English.

Bugs fixed:
- Albums with more than 50 tracks now list every track.
- Recently Played is always listed most recent first and skips local files
  that Spotify cannot play.
- Reopened local-audio transcripts now use the original file instead of a
  stale localhost playback URL, preserve partial text while resuming, and can
  translate partial text after transcription is stopped.
- The transcript view list now always offers Translation when DeepL is
  configured; choosing it fetches the translation. A finished transcription
  also picks up a translation saved earlier.
- Tab and Shift+Tab in the transcript window now visit the same controls;
  previously Tab from the transcript returned to the view list and Shift+Tab
  skipped it.
- Prevented the idle Spotify player from announcing "No tracks" whenever a
  directly streamed podcast starts.
- Split locally generated Whisper transcripts into shorter, word-aligned timed
  lines so playback from the transcript cursor no longer starts up to roughly
  30 seconds before the selected words. Existing coarse Whisper caches are
  regenerated automatically when reopened.


2026.9.5 - 23 September 2026

Features implemented:
- Triple J Hottest 100, ABC Classic 100 and ABC Radio National Top Books
  countdown browsing from official ABC sources.
- Spotify classical-work matching, alternate-recording browsing and Wikipedia
  artist stories.
- Continuous playback from a selected lyric with Space, phrase playback with
  Shift+Space, and pause or resume with Enter in the Lyrics window.

Bugs fixed:
- Kept translated lyrics synchronized with playback and retained the reader's
  approximate position when switching between original and translated lyrics.


2026.9.4 - 23 September 2026

Features implemented:
- Search within lyrics and optional DeepL lyric translation.
- Previous and next lyric-section shortcuts.
- Detailed album and recording information and Wikipedia song stories.
- New-release searches and notifications for followed artists and authors.
- Historical number-one singles and albums for several countries.
- Triple J Hottest 100 annual and special countdown browsing from the official
  ABC archive.
- ABC Classic 100 countdown browsing, with complete-album Spotify matching and
  playback for classical works.
- ABC Radio National Top Books browsing for the 2025 Top 100, ranks 101–200,
  and combined Top 200, with on-demand Spotify audiobook matching.
- Named carts for quickly playing saved tracks.
- Bookmarks, concerts, account settings and preferences moved into File.

Bugs fixed:
- Improved Lyrics window focus, tab order, closing and keyboard handling.
- Reused loaded lyrics instead of requesting them again for lyric navigation.
- Added a controlled retry for temporary LRCLIB 503 errors.
- Improved Spotify request, playback and window reliability.
